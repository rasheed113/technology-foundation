# ERGS Dynamics Services Inquiry Specification

## Purpose

Help visitors identify the engineering services they are interested in and guide them toward the appropriate solution.

## Layout

Responsive Service Selection Section

## Available Services

### Software Engineering

Custom software systems and business applications.

### Mobile Applications

Modern Android and cross-platform mobile solutions.

### AI Solutions

Artificial intelligence, machine learning, and intelligent automation.

### Automation Systems

Workflow automation and business process optimization.

### Cloud Systems

Cloud architecture, deployment, infrastructure, and scalability.

## Inquiry Information

Each inquiry should capture:

- Selected Service
- Project Goal
- Estimated Timeline
- Budget Range (Optional)
- Preferred Contact Method

## Design Principles

- Clear service presentation
- Simple selection process
- Responsive layout
- Accessible interface
- Consistent spacing
- Engineering-first identity
