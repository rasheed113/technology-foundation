# ERGS Dynamics Contact Information Specification

## Purpose

Provide clear and accessible communication information for clients, partners, and visitors.

## Layout

Responsive Contact Information Card Grid

## Information Cards

### Email

Primary business communication channel for inquiries, partnerships, and support.

### Phone

Direct communication option for important discussions and collaboration.

### Location

Company location information to establish professional presence.

### Working Hours

Availability information for communication and response expectations.

## Information Principles

Each contact method should communicate:

- Purpose
- Availability
- Professional usage
- Clear action path

## Design Principles

- Premium information cards
- Clear communication hierarchy
- Responsive layout
- Accessible presentation
- Consistent spacing
- Brand identity focused
