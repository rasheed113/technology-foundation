# ERGS Dynamics Hiring Process Specification

## Purpose

Explain the structured hiring journey used to identify talented engineers, researchers, and technology professionals.

## Layout

Premium Timeline Process Section

## Hiring Stages

### 1. Application

Candidates share their experience, skills, and interest in joining ERGS Dynamics.

### 2. Initial Review

Reviewing background, technical experience, and alignment with company values.

### 3. Technical Discussion

Understanding technical knowledge, problem-solving approach, and engineering mindset.

### 4. Engineering Assessment

Evaluating practical skills through relevant technical challenges.

### 5. Final Interview

Discussing collaboration, responsibilities, and long-term growth opportunities.

### 6. Offer & Onboarding

Welcoming selected candidates and preparing them for successful contribution.

## Process Principles

- Transparent communication
- Fair evaluation
- Engineering-focused assessment
- Respectful candidate experience

- Clear timeline visualization
- Premium process design
- Easy understanding
- Responsive layout
