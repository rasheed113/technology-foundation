## Core Culture Principles

### Engineering Excellence

Building reliable systems through discipline, quality, and continuous improvement.

### Learning & Research

Encouraging experimentation, knowledge sharing, and technical growth.

### Ownership

Taking responsibility for products, systems, and long-term outcomes.

### Collaboration

Working together through communication, trust, and shared goals.
