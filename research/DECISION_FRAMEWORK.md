# Decision Framework

## Purpose

This document defines how important technical and product decisions are evaluated and approved within this technology foundation.

Good decisions are based on evidence, engineering judgment, and long-term thinking.

---

# Decision Principles

Every important decision should be:

- Evidence-based
- Documented
- Transparent
- Reversible when practical
- Aligned with company principles

---

# Decision Process

Before making a major decision:

1. Define the problem.
2. Collect relevant information.
3. Evaluate available options.
4. Assess risks and trade-offs.
5. Select the best solution.
6. Document the reasoning.
7. Review the outcome after implementation.

---

# Evaluation Criteria

Consider:

- User value
- Engineering quality
- Security
- Maintainability
- Performance
- Cost
- Long-term impact

No single factor should dominate every decision.

---

# Trade-offs

Every engineering decision has trade-offs.

Document:

- Benefits
- Risks
- Alternatives considered
- Reason for the final decision

---

# Continuous Review

Decisions should be reviewed when:

- New evidence becomes available
- Product requirements change
- Better solutions emerge

Learning is part of decision-making.

---

# Final Principle

A documented decision is better than an undocumented assumption.

Good decisions create strong products.
