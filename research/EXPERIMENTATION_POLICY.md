# Experimentation Policy

## Purpose

This document defines how experiments are planned, executed, evaluated, and documented within this technology foundation.

Experiments help validate ideas before they become permanent solutions.

---

# Experiment Principles

Every experiment should be:

- Purpose-driven
- Documented
- Measurable
- Reproducible
- Ethical

Experiments should reduce uncertainty, not create confusion.

---

# Before an Experiment

Clearly define:

- The problem
- The hypothesis
- Success criteria
- Risks
- Expected outcomes

---

# Running the Experiment

During execution:

- Record observations
- Measure results
- Avoid changing multiple variables at once
- Preserve important data

Consistency improves reliability.

---

# Evaluation

After completion, evaluate:

- Did the hypothesis hold?
- What evidence was collected?
- What worked?
- What failed?
- What should change next?

---

# Documentation

Every significant experiment should record:

- Objective
- Method
- Results
- Conclusions
- Follow-up actions

Documented experiments become organizational knowledge.

---

# Learning

Every experiment provides value.

Successful experiments validate ideas.

Unsuccessful experiments eliminate weak ideas.

Both outcomes improve engineering decisions.

---

# Final Principle

An experiment is successful when it produces reliable knowledge, regardless of whether the original hypothesis is confirmed.
