# Business Continuity

## Purpose

This document defines the principles for maintaining essential business operations during disruptions and restoring normal operations as efficiently as possible.

Business continuity protects users, services, data, and organizational stability.

---

# Business Continuity Philosophy

Disruptions are inevitable.

Prepared organizations anticipate disruption, reduce operational impact, and continue delivering critical services whenever possible.

Business continuity is an essential part of responsible engineering.

---

# Objectives

Business continuity aims to:

- Maintain critical operations
- Protect users
- Preserve essential data
- Reduce downtime
- Support operational resilience
- Enable rapid recovery

Preparation reduces the impact of unexpected events.

---

# Critical Functions

The organization should identify and prioritize critical functions, including:

- Product availability
- User support
- Data access
- Infrastructure operations
- Security monitoring
- Engineering collaboration

Critical functions should receive the highest continuity priority.

---

# Continuity Planning

Business continuity planning should include:

- Risk assessment
- Critical system identification
- Backup strategies
- Alternative operating procedures
- Communication plans
- Recovery priorities

Plans should remain practical and regularly reviewed.

---

# Roles and Responsibilities

Business continuity requires coordinated effort across the organization.

Engineering teams, leadership, operations, and support personnel should understand their responsibilities before disruptions occur.

Prepared teams respond more effectively.

---

# Testing and Review

Continuity plans should be periodically tested through:

- Simulations
- Tabletop exercises
- Recovery drills
- Process reviews

Testing validates preparedness and identifies opportunities for improvement.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Business continuity should evolve through:

- Incident reviews
- Operational experience
- Engineering feedback
- Research
- Risk assessments

Continuous refinement improves organizational resilience.

---

# Final Principle

Business continuity is not measured by the absence of disruption.

It is measured by the organization's ability to continue serving users, protect critical operations, and recover with confidence.
