# Compliance Principles

## Purpose

This document defines the principles for ensuring that products, engineering practices, and organizational operations comply with applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, ethical, and internal requirements.

Compliance strengthens trust, reduces organizational risk, and supports sustainable growth.

---

# Compliance Philosophy

Compliance should be integrated into engineering and operational processes rather than treated as a final checklist.

Responsible organizations build compliance into the entire product lifecycle.

---

# Objectives

Compliance aims to:

- Protect users
- Support legal and regulatory obligations
- Maintain organizational integrity
- Reduce operational risk
- Promote responsible engineering
- Strengthen stakeholder confidence

Compliance supports long-term success.

---

# Scope

Compliance considerations may include:

- Applicable laws
- Industry regulations
- Security standards
- Privacy requirements
- Contractual obligations
- Organizational policies
- Ethical principles

Requirements should be reviewed throughout the lifecycle of every significant product.

---

# Engineering Responsibility

Every engineering team shares responsibility for compliance.

Compliance should be considered during:

- Planning
- Design
- Development
- Testing
- Deployment
- Maintenance
- Product retirement

Responsibility is shared across the organization.

---

# Documentation

Compliance activities should be supported by appropriate documentation, including:

- Policies
- Standards
- Procedures
- Risk assessments
- Audit records
- Change history

Good documentation improves accountability and traceability.

---

# Monitoring and Review

Compliance should be continuously monitored through:

- Internal reviews
- Engineering audits
- Security assessments
- Documentation reviews
- Process evaluations

Regular review helps identify improvement opportunities.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Compliance practices should evolve through:

- Regulatory changes
- Engineering experience
- Incident reviews
- Research
- Organizational learning

Continuous improvement ensures long-term effectiveness.

---

# Final Principle

Compliance is not simply about meeting requirements.

It is about building trustworthy systems through responsible engineering, transparency, and continuous accountability.
