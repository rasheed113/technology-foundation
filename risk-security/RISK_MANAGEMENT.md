# Risk Management

## Purpose

This document establishes the principles for identifying, assessing, mitigating, monitoring, and continuously managing risks across all technology products, engineering activities, and organizational operations.

Effective risk management supports resilience, protects users, and enables sustainable innovation.

---

# Risk Philosophy

Risk cannot be completely eliminated.

Instead, risks should be understood, evaluated, communicated, and managed through responsible engineering and informed decision-making.

Managing risk is a continuous responsibility shared across the organization.

---

# Final Principle

Risk management is not about avoiding innovation.

It is about enabling innovation responsibly through preparation, awareness, and disciplined engineering.
