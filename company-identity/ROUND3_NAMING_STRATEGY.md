# Round 3 Naming Strategy

## Purpose

Round 3 introduces a stricter naming process.

Lessons learned:

- Valora rejected due to existing commercial usage.
- Aetram rejected due to existing commercial usage.
- Corvix rejected due to existing commercial usage.

## New Selection Rules

A candidate should:

- Be an original invented name.
- Pass initial web conflict screening.
- Support long-term global branding.
- Fit a multi-product technology company.
- Be easy to pronounce internationally.

## Process

1. Generate new candidates.
2. Initial conflict screening.
3. Score shortlisted candidates.
4. Domain review.
5. Trademark review.
6. Final approval.
