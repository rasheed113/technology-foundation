# Company Constitution

## Preamble

This Constitution establishes the permanent principles that govern our organization, our engineering culture, and every product we create.

It exists to ensure that growth never compromises integrity, innovation never compromises responsibility, and success never compromises trust.

Every engineering decision, product decision, research effort, and public release shall remain consistent with these principles.

---

# Constitutional Authority

This Constitution is the highest governing document after the Foundation Charter.

All standards, policies, products, and future engineering decisions derive their authority from this Constitution.

No person, team, product, feature, business objective, or financial interest is above this Constitution.

> No one is above the Constitution.

---

# Permanent Principles

## Principle 1 — Trust Before Profit

We prioritize long-term user trust above short-term financial gain.

---

## Principle 2 — Documentation Before Development

Important engineering work begins with documentation before implementation.

---

## Principle 3 — Research Before Release

Every major feature must be supported by research, testing, and validation before public release.

---

## Principle 4 — Quality Before Speed

Fast delivery must never compromise reliability or maintainability.

---

## Principle 5 — Security by Design

Security is a design requirement, not an afterthought.

---

## Principle 6 — Performance with Responsibility

Efficient software respects both user time and system resources.

---

## Principle 7 — Continuous Learning

Every mistake becomes documented knowledge for future improvement.

---

## Principle 8 — Transparency

We communicate honestly about limitations, risks, failures, and improvements.

---

## Principle 9 — Long-Term Thinking

Engineering decisions should remain valuable years into the future.

---

## Principle 10 — Zero Ego Engineering

Ideas compete through evidence, testing, and reasoning—not titles or opinions.

---

## Principle 11 — Technology Must Serve People

Technology exists to empower people, improve decisions, and solve meaningful problems.

People should never become servants of technology.

---

# Legacy Principle

Leave every product, document, system, and decision better than it was found.

Every generation of engineers should strengthen the Foundation rather than weaken it.

---

# Constitutional Promise

We will make mistakes.

When we do, we will acknowledge them, learn from them, and improve our products.

---

# Closing Statement

This Constitution defines who we are, how we work, and why we build technology.

It is intended to guide not only today's products but every future product created under this Foundation.

---

> Every line of code carries responsibility.
