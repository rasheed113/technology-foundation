# Legacy Principle

## Introduction

Technology is temporary.

Legacy is permanent.

Our goal is not simply to build successful products.

Our goal is to leave behind knowledge, principles, systems, and products that continue creating value long after they are released.

---

# Our Legacy

Every product should leave people better informed.

Every document should leave engineers better prepared.

Every improvement should strengthen the Foundation.

Every release should increase trust.

Every generation of engineers should inherit stronger standards than the generation before.

---

# Continuous Improvement

We never assume our work is perfect.

We improve through research.

We improve through testing.

We improve through experience.

We improve through humility.

Every lesson learned becomes part of our collective knowledge.

---

# Responsibility to the Future

Engineering decisions should not only solve today's problems.

They should also reduce tomorrow's problems.

Long-term thinking is one of the greatest responsibilities of engineering.

---

# Zero Ego Legacy

Our legacy belongs to the Foundation, not to individuals.

Ideas survive because they are correct.

Not because of who proposed them.

Evidence, research, and integrity will always outweigh titles, opinions, or authority.

---

# Final Commitment

We measure our legacy not by the number of products we release,

but by the trust we leave behind.

When future engineers study our work,

they should inherit stronger principles,

better documentation,

higher standards,

and greater responsibility.

That is our legacy.

---

> Leave every product, document, system, and decision better than you found it.

> Every line of code carries responsibility.
