# Engineering Manifesto

## Introduction

This Manifesto defines the beliefs that guide our engineering decisions.

Technology changes.

Programming languages evolve.

Tools become obsolete.

Principles endure.

---

# We Believe

We believe that trust is earned through consistency.

We believe that quality is designed, not inspected.

We believe that documentation is an engineering asset.

We believe that research reduces unnecessary risk.

We believe that testing protects users.

We believe that simplicity is stronger than unnecessary complexity.

We believe that transparency builds confidence.

We believe that learning never ends.

---

# We Choose

We choose discipline over shortcuts.

We choose evidence over assumptions.

We choose responsibility over hype.

We choose long-term value over temporary success.

We choose continuous improvement over complacency.

---

# We Reject

We reject engineering based on guesswork.

We reject releasing unfinished products.

We reject compromising user trust for business pressure.

We reject unnecessary complexity.

We reject ego over evidence.

---

# Our Standard

Every product should have a reason to exist.

Every feature should solve a real problem.

Every release should improve quality.

Every mistake should become knowledge.

Every engineer should leave the system better than they found it.

---

# Our Responsibility

We do not measure success only by downloads, revenue, or popularity.

We measure success by the trust our users place in our products.

Trust is our greatest achievement.

---

# Closing Statement

Engineering is not simply the process of writing software.

Engineering is the discipline of building technology worthy of people's trust.

---

> Success is not our first goal.

> Deserving success is.
