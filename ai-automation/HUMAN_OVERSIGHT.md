# Human Oversight

## Purpose

This document defines the role of human oversight in the design, deployment, and operation of Artificial Intelligence and automation systems.

Human oversight ensures that technology remains aligned with ethical principles, organizational values, and user interests.

---

# Core Principle

Artificial Intelligence should support human decision-making rather than replace it.

Humans remain responsible for decisions that have significant impact on users, products, or the organization.

---

# Human-in-the-Loop

Where appropriate, AI systems should operate with a Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) approach.

This means that qualified individuals review, approve, or intervene in important decisions before action is taken.

---

# Decision Authority

Final authority remains with authorized human personnel.

AI may:

- Provide recommendations
- Analyze information
- Identify patterns
- Suggest actions

AI should not independently make high-impact decisions without appropriate human review.

---

# Review Requirements

Human review should be considered for:

- High-risk operations
- Sensitive user interactions
- Security-related actions
- Financial decisions
- Legal or regulatory matters
- Significant architectural changes

The level of review should be proportional to the potential impact.

---

# Escalation

When an AI system encounters uncertainty, conflicting information, or unexpected situations, it should defer to human judgment.

Escalation procedures should be clearly defined and documented.

---

# Accountability

Responsibility cannot be delegated to AI.

Engineers, project owners, and organizational leadership remain accountable for the systems they develop and deploy.

---

# Continuous Monitoring

Human oversight includes ongoing evaluation of AI systems through:

- Performance reviews
- Operational monitoring
- Incident analysis
- User feedback
- Periodic audits

Continuous oversight supports long-term reliability.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Lessons learned through oversight should be incorporated into future system improvements.

Regular review strengthens both AI systems and engineering practices.

---

# Final Principle

Artificial Intelligence should amplify human capability, but responsibility always remains with people.

Technology serves humanity best when human judgment remains central to important decisions.
