# AI Governance

## Purpose

This document establishes the governance framework for the responsible development, deployment, operation, and continuous improvement of Artificial Intelligence systems across this technology foundation.

AI Governance ensures that AI remains aligned with engineering principles, organizational values, legal obligations, and user trust.

---

# Governance Principles

AI governance should be:

- Responsible
- Transparent
- Accountable
- Secure
- Human-centered
- Evidence-based

Governance provides structure without limiting innovation.

---

# Scope

This framework applies to:

- AI-assisted software
- Machine learning systems
- Automation platforms
- Decision-support systems
- Internal engineering tools
- Future AI technologies adopted by the organization

All AI initiatives should follow these governance principles.

---

# Roles and Responsibilities

Successful AI governance requires collaboration.

Responsibilities include:

- Engineering teams building reliable systems
- Project owners defining objectives
- Leadership providing strategic direction
- Human reviewers overseeing important decisions
- Security teams protecting AI infrastructure
- Quality teams validating system behavior

Governance is a shared responsibility.

---

# Risk Management

AI systems should undergo appropriate risk assessment before deployment.

Risk evaluation should consider:

- User impact
- Reliability
- Security
- Privacy
- Fairness
- Operational stability

Higher-risk systems require stronger oversight.

---

# Documentation

Every significant AI system should maintain documentation describing:

- Purpose
- Design decisions
- Data sources
- Known limitations
- Testing results
- Version history
- Governance decisions

Good documentation supports transparency and long-term maintainability.

---

# Monitoring and Auditing

AI systems should be regularly monitored for:

- Performance
- Reliability
- Unexpected behavior
- Security concerns
- Compliance with organizational standards

Periodic audits help maintain confidence in AI systems.

---

# Continuous Improvement

AI governance should evolve through:

- Research
- Engineering experience
- Incident reviews
- User feedback
- Technological advancements
- Regular policy reviews

Continuous learning strengthens governance over time.

---

# Compliance

AI systems should comply with applicable organizational policies, legal requirements, and ethical standards.

Compliance should be considered throughout the entire lifecycle of an AI system.

---

# Final Principle

Artificial Intelligence is most valuable when guided by responsible governance.

Strong governance enables innovation while protecting users, supporting engineers, and building long-term trust.
