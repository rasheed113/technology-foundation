# AI Principles

## Purpose

This document defines the core principles for designing, developing, and using Artificial Intelligence within this technology foundation.

AI should enhance human capability through responsible, transparent, and trustworthy engineering.

---

# Core Principles

Our AI systems should be:

- Helpful
- Reliable
- Transparent
- Responsible
- Human-centered

AI exists to assist people, not replace human judgment.

---

# Human-Centered Design

AI should empower users by:

- Supporting better decisions
- Reducing repetitive work
- Improving productivity
- Respecting human control

Technology should always serve people.

---

# Transparency

Users should understand:

- When AI is being used
- What AI can do
- What AI cannot do
- The limitations of AI-generated output

Transparency builds trust.

---

# Reliability

AI systems should be:

- Tested
- Monitored
- Continuously improved
- Used within their intended purpose

Reliability is earned through disciplined engineering.

---

# Responsible Use

AI should never be used to intentionally deceive, manipulate, or harm people.

Every AI feature should align with our engineering principles and company values.

---

# Continuous Improvement

AI systems should improve through:

- Research
- Evaluation
- User feedback
- Responsible iteration

Learning never stops.

---

# Final Principle

Artificial Intelligence is a tool to support human intelligence.

The best AI strengthens human capability while preserving human responsibility.
