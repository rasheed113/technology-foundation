# Responsible AI

## Purpose

This document defines the principles for the ethical and responsible development and use of Artificial Intelligence within this technology foundation.

Responsible AI protects users, promotes trust, and supports long-term innovation.

---

# Core Principles

AI should always be:

- Ethical
- Transparent
- Accountable
- Reliable
- Human-centered

Responsible AI is an engineering responsibility.

---

# User Protection

AI systems should never intentionally:

- Mislead users
- Manipulate decisions
- Produce harmful outcomes
- Violate user trust

Protecting users is a primary responsibility.

---

# Transparency

Users should know:

- When AI is involved
- The purpose of the AI system
- Important limitations
- When human review may be required

Transparency supports informed decisions.

---

# Accountability

Humans remain responsible for important decisions.

AI provides recommendations and assistance but does not replace human accountability.

---

# Fairness

AI systems should be designed to reduce unnecessary bias and support fair treatment wherever practical.

Fairness should be considered throughout development and evaluation.

---

# Monitoring

AI systems should be monitored for:

- Reliability
- Unexpected behavior
- Performance
- Opportunities for improvement

Monitoring supports continuous quality.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Responsible AI evolves through:

- Research
- Testing
- Feedback
- Engineering reviews

Learning is continuous.

---

# Final Principle

Responsible AI is not only about building intelligent systems.

It is about building systems that deserve trust.
