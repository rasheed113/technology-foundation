# Product Lifecycle

## Purpose

This document defines the complete lifecycle of every product developed under this technology foundation.

Every product should follow a consistent, disciplined, and well-documented lifecycle from idea to retirement.

---

# Lifecycle Overview

Every product progresses through the following stages:

1. Vision
2. Research
3. Planning
4. Requirements
5. Architecture
6. Development
7. Testing
8. Release
9. Maintenance
10. Evolution
11. Retirement

---

# Final Principle

A successful product is not defined only by its launch.

It is defined by the value it creates throughout its entire lifecycle.
